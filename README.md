# GraphNPE

**GraphNPE for inferring dark matter density profiles in dwarf galaxies**

This repository implements the GraphNPE method described in [Nguyen et al. (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.12825) and [Nguyen et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03812), which uses graph neural networks combined with simulation-based inference to recover dark matter density profiles from stellar kinematics in dwarf galaxies.

## Method

GraphNPE infers posterior distributions on dark matter density profiles directly from line-of-sight stellar velocities, without relying on Jeans modeling summary statistics such as the velocity dispersion. The method is trained on mock data generated by the [Agama](https://github.com/GalacticDynamics-Oxford/Agama) library. The method has been tested on FIRE-2 simulations and works for both CDM and SIDM scenarios (see Nguyen et al. (2025)).

## Usage

The pipeline consists of three stages:

1. **Train embedding network**: `python train_embed.py --config=configs/embed.py`
2. **Train density estimator**: `python train_npe.py --config=configs/npe.py`
3. **Run inference**: Use the trained models to obtain posterior samples on DM parameters

Step (1) and (2) can be merged into a single step. See the paper for details on the methodology and validation.
